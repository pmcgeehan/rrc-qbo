RRC Board Reporting (read-only)

RRC Board Reporting is a private, read-only connection to the Rafael Racquet Club's QuickBooks Online company. A member of the Club's Board uses it to prepare monthly financial reviews, budgets and summaries for the Board and members.

It is not available to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for here. The connection is set up and used on the operator's own computer.

If you reached this page from QuickBooks after connecting, the connection is complete. You can close this window.

Read-only

The tool can read reports and records. It cannot create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.

Disconnecting

The Club can disconnect it at any time in QuickBooks Online under Apps → My apps.
