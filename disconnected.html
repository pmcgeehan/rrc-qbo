Disconnected from QuickBooks

RRC Board Reporting is no longer connected to the Club's QuickBooks Online company. It can no longer read any of the Club's data.

The operator will delete the stored connection credentials. To reconnect later, the operator signs in again through Intuit with an authorized Club login.

Nothing in QuickBooks was changed by disconnecting. The tool was read-only throughout.
