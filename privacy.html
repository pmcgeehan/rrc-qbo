RRC Board Reporting (read-only)

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains how RRC Board Reporting (the "App") handles data from the Rafael Racquet Club's (the "Club") QuickBooks Online company. The App is a private tool operated by a member of the Club's Board of Directors (the "Operator") for the Club's internal financial reporting. It is not offered to the public.

What data the App reads

With the Club's authorization, the App reads the Club's QuickBooks Online accounting data, read-only:

  • financial reports such as Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Budget vs. Actuals and aging reports;
  • accounts, budgets and transactions, including the names of customers (members) and vendors as they appear in the Club's books;
  • basic company information (company name and ID).

The App cannot create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks. It does not access payment card data, bank login credentials or Intuit account passwords.

Why the data is used

Only to prepare the Club's own financial reporting: monthly financial reviews, budget-versus-actual analysis, budgets and plans, and financial summaries for the Club's Board and members. Reports shared with members contain totals only; individual member names and balances are not included in them.

AI processing

The App uses Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to read QuickBooks data and help prepare these reports. Data retrieved by the App is therefore sent to Anthropic for processing, under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). It is used only to produce the Club's reports.

Where data is kept

  • Report exports and working files are kept on the Operator's computer.
  • Finished reports are shared with the Club's Board and members through the Club's usual channels (for example Google Sheets and Google Docs) and, where the Board chooses, an online dashboard visible only to people it is shared with.
  • The connection's access credentials (refresh token and company ID) and the App's keys are stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain on the Operator's computer.

Sharing

QuickBooks data is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone outside the Club except Anthropic for the AI processing described above. Intuit receives the API requests the App makes, as part of providing QuickBooks Online.

Retention

Working copies are kept for as long as they are useful for the Club's reporting and are deleted when no longer needed, or when the Club asks.

Security

Access is read-only and limited to the Club's own company. Credentials are encrypted at rest, connections to Intuit use HTTPS, and only one authorized person operates the App.

Disconnecting

The Club can disconnect the App at any time in QuickBooks Online (Apps → My apps) or by asking the Operator, who will then delete the stored credentials. After that the App cannot read any further data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
