RRC Board Reporting (the "App") is a private, internal tool used to prepare financial reports for the Rafael Racquet Club (the "Club") from the Club's QuickBooks Online company. It is operated by a member of the Club's Board of Directors (the "Operator"). It is not offered to the public and is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store.

1. Who may use the App

The App may be connected only to the Club's own QuickBooks Online company, by a person authorized by the Club to do so. No other company, organization or person is licensed to use it.

2. What the App does

The App reads the Club's accounting data through Intuit's QuickBooks Online Accounting API, with read-only access. It retrieves reports and records (for example Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and Budget vs. Actuals) so the Operator can prepare monthly financial reviews, budgets and summaries for the Club's Board and members. It does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.

The Club is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the App for its own internal financial reporting. The App may not be resold, sublicensed or used to provide services to anyone else.

4. Connecting and disconnecting

The App connects to QuickBooks only after an authorized Club user approves the connection through Intuit's sign-in. The Club can disconnect the App at any time from QuickBooks Online (Apps → My apps) or by asking the Operator to revoke it. Once disconnected, the App can no longer read the Club's data.
